    01

    Where to use the 1200x1000 heavy pallet

    1200 x 1000 mm is one of the six pallet sizes in the international ISO 6780 standard and many people call it the industrial pallet format. Industry and companies in the United Kingdom use it widely. In addition, it matches the CP1 and CP6 formats of the chemical industry. What is more, a plastic pallet needs no heat treatment or stamp under the ISPM 15 standard for wood packaging. That makes this 1200x1000 heavy pallet a practical choice for both internal logistics and export.

    02

    Load capacity: 7,500 kg static, 1,500 kg dynamic and 1,250 kg racking

    The static load of 7,500 kg applies when the pallet rests on a flat, level floor, for example in block stacking. In contrast, the dynamic load of 1,500 kg applies when a forklift or pallet truck lifts and moves the loaded pallet. Finally, the racking load of 1,250 kg applies when only the rack beams support the pallet.

    All values assume an evenly distributed load. For the racking load, Q-Pall uses a test protocol with 25 kg bags. Point loads or uneven loading reduce the usable capacity, so always check the pallet against your actual load.

    03

    Open deck and three runners

    The open deck lets moisture drain away and allows air to circulate around the load. Three runners give the pallet a stable base on the floor and on rack beams. Its weight of 20.3 kg reflects a robust construction for repeated use. The pallet consists of a mix of HDPE and PP, does not absorb moisture and has no nails or splinters.

    04

    Other versions of the 1200x1000 heavy pallet

    Would you prefer a different configuration? Then consider the heavy version with a closed deck and three runners, which weighs 24.1 kg and carries 7,500 kg static, 1,500 kg dynamic and 1,375 kg in racking. Alternatively, the heavy version with an open deck and six runners weighs 21.7 kg and carries 7,500 kg static, 1,500 kg dynamic and 1,375 kg in racking. In addition, the heavy version with a closed deck and six runners weighs 25.2 kg and carries 7,500 kg static, 1,500 kg dynamic and 1,375 kg in racking.
