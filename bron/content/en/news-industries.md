> **In short**
>
> * **Food:** hygienic pallets made of virgin plastic, smooth and easy to clean.
> * **Retail:** display pallets of 600 x 400 and 600 x 800 mm, straight into the shop without repacking.
> * **Export:** plastic export pallets are always light and exempt from ISPM 15.
> * **Chemicals and industry:** CP pallets for bagged goods, drums, octabins and big bags.
> * **Beverages and pools:** the Ultra pallet, a heavy pallet built for many trips.

## Every industry has its own requirements

Plastic pallets are used in more and more industries, because they do not absorb moisture, have no splinters or nails and last a long time. Even so, not every pallet suits every use and every load. That is why we list, per industry, the pallets that work best.

## Food industry: hygienic pallets

In the food industry, a pallet must never be a source of contamination. If you work to HACCP, you want to keep the risk of contamination as low as possible. A [hygienic pallet](@type-hygienic) helps with that.

This pallet has a smooth design that is as closed as possible, without seams and corners where dirt can build up. It does not absorb moisture and can withstand intensive cleaning. Our hygienic pallets are made of virgin plastic, the standard for direct contact with food.

Is the food packaged rather than lying directly on the pallet? Then our recycled plastic pallets are a sustainable choice.

## Retail: display pallets

Supermarkets and shops make extensive use of [display pallets](@type-display). The product is loaded onto the pallet in the distribution centre and goes onto the shop floor without repacking. That saves time and prevents damage.

Our display pallets come in 600 x 400 mm (quarter Euro pallet) and 600 x 800 mm (half Euro pallet). They are light and nestable, so empty pallets take up little space on the return trip.

## Export: export pallets without ISPM 15

If you export abroad, you face many rules, including for pallets. Under ISPM 15, wooden pallets must be heat-treated and stamped. Plastic pallets are exempt.

With plastic [export pallets](@type-export), you therefore do not need to arrange treatment or obtain a treatment certificate. No document can expire if your shipment unexpectedly takes longer. Our export pallets are always light pallets: light, often nestable and strong enough for the journey.

## Chemicals and industry: CP pallets

The [CP pallet](@type-cp) (Chemical Pallet) was originally developed for the European chemical and plastics industry. Today, many other industries use it too. Think of drums of liquids, bagged goods, octabins and big bags with powder or granules.

We make the popular CP1, CP3, CP6 and CP9 in plastic. A plastic CP pallet has no nails or loose boards that could damage your bags or drums.

## Beverages and pools: the Ultra pallet

In the beverage industry, pallets often circulate many times, for example in a pool. That is what the [Ultra pallet](@ultra) is made for: a heavy pallet of 1200 x 1000 mm with anti-slip on the top and bottom. It carries up to 1,375 kg in racking.
