## Sturdy, dimensionally stable and four-way entry

Block pallets are the workhorses of industry. Thanks to their sturdy construction, you can pick them up with a forklift from all four sides. Q-Pall plastic block pallets have consistent dimensions and a fixed weight. So they are suitable for automated warehouses, roller conveyors and robots.

## Medium and heavy

- **Medium:** 5,000 to 6,000 kg static, 1,250 to 1,500 kg dynamic, 750 to 1,375 kg in racking. On 3 or 5 runners, with open or closed deck.
- **Heavy:** 7,500 kg static, 1,500 kg dynamic and up to 1,375 kg in racking. On 3, 5 or 6 runners.

## New and used

Besides new block pallets, Q-Pall also has [used plastic pallets](@used). These are inspected and a good choice if you want to save costs and work in a circular way.
