## Space for bulk goods

At 1200 x 1200 mm, you have plenty of surface for large, heavy or round packaging. Big bags, drums and IBCs stand stable and the weight is distributed well across the deck. The size is widely used in the [chemical industry](@sector-chemical), the food industry, pharmaceuticals and agriculture.

## The range

- **Light:** nestable with 9 feet, or with 3 runners. 2,500 kg static and 1,000 kg dynamic.
- **Medium:** open deck with 3 or 6 runners, 7,500 kg static and 1,375 kg dynamic.
- **Heavy:** open or closed deck with 3 or 6 runners, 7,500 kg static, 2,000 kg dynamic and 1,375 kg in racking.

Separate [1200 mm runners](@acc-runners) are available for the nestable pallet. A [1200 x 1200 mm pallet collar](@acc-collars) turns the pallet into a sturdy, reusable box.
