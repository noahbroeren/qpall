## The half Euro pallet

At 600 x 800 mm, this pallet has exactly half the surface area of a Euro pallet. The size is widely used for displays in supermarkets, for promotions and for smaller shipments that still need to be moved with a forklift or pallet truck.

## Three versions

- **Light with 3 runners:** the lightest version, suitable for [export](@type-export) and one-off shipments.
- **Medium with 6 feet:** 3.2 kg, 1,000 kg static and 500 kg dynamic. Good value for money.
- **Heavy with 6 feet:** 5.9 kg, 1,000 kg static and 600 kg dynamic, for more intensive use.

All models are nestable and are packed in quantities of 80 or 100. A matching [600 x 800 mm pallet collar](@acc-collars) and a [plastic lid](@acc-lids) turn it into a closed transport box.
