## Circular and cost-effective

With normal use, a plastic pallet lasts for many years. Even when a customer takes a pallet out of circulation, it is often still perfectly usable. With Q-Used, we buy these pallets, inspect them and sell them again. No new raw material is needed.

## Benefits of used plastic pallets

- **Up to 40% cheaper** than a new pallet.
- **Inspected:** every pallet is checked for damage and its construction.
- **Dimensionally stable:** plastic does not warp from moisture.
- **Export-ready:** like new plastic pallets, exempt from [ISPM 15](@guide-ispm).
- **Hygienic and safe:** no rust, splinters or loose parts.

## How it works

1. Contact us with the size, load and quantity you are looking for.
2. We let you know what is in stock and send you a quote.
3. The pallets are delivered from our warehouse in Veghel.
