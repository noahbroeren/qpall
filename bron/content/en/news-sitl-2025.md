> **In short**
>
> * Q-Pall exhibited at SITL 2025 in Paris, the trade fair for transport and logistics, together with partner BacLand.
> * The theme was "Connecting Our Intelligence".
> * We showed our range in three load classes: light, medium and heavy.
> * The focus was on smart design, recycled plastic and local production.

## What we showed

Logistics is changing fast. Companies are looking for smart, sustainable solutions, and the pallet is part of that. At SITL 2025, together with our partner BacLand, we showed how smart design, recycled plastic and local production come together in our pallets.

On the stand we presented our range in three load classes, from light pallets for distribution to models for the toughest applications:

* **Light.** [Light plastic pallets](@class-light), often nestable. For export, one-off shipments and distribution.
* **Medium.** [Medium plastic pallets](@class-medium) are the all-rounders for internal transport and light racking loads.
* **Heavy.** [Heavy-duty pallets](@class-heavy) are built for intensive use, high dynamic loads and pallet racking, up to 7,500 kg static.

## The story behind the stand

We make our standard pallets from almost 100% recycled plastic, a mix of HDPE and PP. We produce them in six factories across Europe, so almost every customer is within 600 kilometres of a factory. Moulds travel, pallets don't: we place the mould in the factory closest to the customer. Read more about [local production](@about-local).

Visitors could talk to our team about their own challenges: cutting CO₂ emissions, automating processes or moving goods more efficiently. Plastic pallets are dimensionally stable and have a constant weight. That makes them well suited to [automation and AGVs](@guide-automation).
