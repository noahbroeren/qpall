## Plastic pallets for every application

Q-Pall develops and produces only plastic pallets, and has done so since 2005. You can see that focus in the range: around 125 versions in nine sizes, from the compact [600 x 400 mm display pallet](@size-600x400) to the large [1300 x 1100 mm pallet for the chemical industry](@size-1300x1100). Each model is designed for a clear task: transport, storage, export, pooling or automated logistics.

### Choose by size first

The size determines whether the pallet fits your racking, trucks and containers. In Europe, the [1200 x 800 mm Euro pallet](@size-1200x800) is the standard. The [1200 x 1000 mm industrial pallet](@size-1200x1000) is popular in industry and in the United Kingdom. Square sizes such as [1100 x 1100 mm](@size-1100x1100) and [1140 x 1140 mm](@size-1140x1140) are made for sea containers and big bags.

### Then choose by load capacity

Every pallet has three load values: static (on the floor), dynamic (during transport with a forklift or pallet truck) and racking load (on two beams in pallet racking). On our page about [the load capacity of plastic pallets](@about-load) we explain the difference.

### Open or closed deck, feet or runners

An **open deck** is lighter and lets moisture and air through. A **closed deck** is easier to clean and gives small packaging a flat base. Pallets with **9 feet** are often nestable and ideal for export. Pallets with **3, 5 or 6 runners** are more stable on roller conveyors; in the medium and heavy versions they are also suitable for racking.
