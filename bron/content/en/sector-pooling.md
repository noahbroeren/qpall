## Why plastic in a pool?

In a pool, a pallet circulates dozens or hundreds of times. Then its service life counts for more than the purchase price. Plastic pallets:

- **have fixed dimensions** for automated processes at all participants;
- **have no splinters, nails or warping**, unlike wood;
- **are fully recyclable and circular**;
- **have a consistent weight and consistent quality**;
- **are available directly from stock** in the popular sizes.
