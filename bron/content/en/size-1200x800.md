## The standard for European logistics

1200 x 800 mm is the size of the Euro pallet. According to EPAL, around 675 million wooden Euro pallets are in circulation. Warehouses, pallet racking, trucks and order picking processes are designed around this size. It is also one of the six pallet sizes in the international standard ISO 6780. With a plastic pallet in this size, you simply work within your existing processes, but without splinters, nails and moisture problems.

## Light, medium or heavy in 1200 x 800 mm

### Light: lightweight and nestable for export

The light pallets with 9 feet are nestable. When empty, you slide them into each other, which saves a lot of space in storage and return transport. They weigh around 5 kg and carry 2,500 kg static and 1,000 kg dynamic. A plastic pallet also needs no heat treatment under [ISPM 15](@guide-ispm), so these pallets are ideal for [export](@type-export). Do you want extra stability? Then fit [click-on runners](@acc-runners) underneath.

### Medium: the all-rounder

The medium pallets with 3 runners are the best choice for internal transport, distribution and light racking storage. They carry 5,000 to 6,000 kg static and up to 750 kg in racking. The [anti-slip version](@type-antislip) with closed deck (since late 2025) provides extra grip on roller conveyors and means less stretch film.

### Heavy: for intensive use and racking storage

For heavy loads and high racking, choose a heavy pallet. These pallets carry up to 7,500 kg static, up to 1,750 kg dynamic and up to 1,750 kg in racking. In this size you will also find the [H1 pallet and hygienic pallets](@type-hygienic) for the food industry.

## Applications of plastic Euro pallets

- **Retail and distribution:** pallets that go directly from the distribution centre to the shop and come back again.
- **Food and pharma:** hygienic pallets with a closed deck that you can clean easily. See [food industry](@sector-food).
- **Beverage industry and pooling:** robust pallets that last for years in a pool. See [pooling](@sector-pooling).
- **Automated warehouses:** thanks to their constant weight and fixed dimensions, plastic pallets cause fewer disruptions on roller conveyors and in AGV systems.
