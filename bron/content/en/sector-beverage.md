## The challenge

The beverage industry has complex logistics, from brewery or bottling plant to distribution all over the world. Pallets must carry heavy loads, be easy to handle and fit into fully automated warehouses. Sustainability, hygiene and safety in the workplace also play a major role.

## Why plastic pallets?

- **Longer service life** than wooden pallets, so less replacement.
- **Resistant to moisture** and easy to clean.
- **No splinters or sharp edges**, safer for employees.
- **Light but strong** for heavy loads.
- **Suitable for automation** and space-saving to stack.
- **Fully recyclable.**

## The Ultra pallet

Together with brands such as Coca-Cola, Heineken, Bavaria, Vrumona, Pepsi, Grolsch and AB InBev, we developed the [Ultra pallet](@ultra): a heavy-duty pallet of 1200 x 1000 x 165 mm with anti-slip on the top and bottom. It carries up to 1,375 kg in pallet racking, without metal reinforcements.

## One million pallets for the Dutch beverage pool

In 2023, we delivered the first pallet of a large order to Drankenpallet Beheer Nederland (the Dutch beverage pallet pool). Up to 2034, we will produce at least one million pallets for this pool at our factory in Balk. Read more about [pooling](@sector-pooling).
