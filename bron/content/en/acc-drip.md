## Drip tray 1200 x 800 mm

The plastic drip tray is 312 mm high and stands on 3 runners. It carries 5,000 kg static and 1,500 kg dynamic. Place drums or jerrycans on the drip tray, and any leaking liquid is collected. This is useful in workshops, storage areas and at production lines.

Please note: specific rules often apply to the storage of hazardous substances, for example regarding the containment volume. Always check which requirements apply to your situation.

## Gratings

- **Galvanised grating:** made of galvanised steel on 3 runners. Liquid, dirt and small residues fall through, so goods stay clean and dry. Resistant to corrosion and suitable for outdoor use.
- **Plastic grid:** a lighter grid on 9 feet that keeps goods off the floor.

Both measure 1200 x 800 mm and therefore fit on the drip tray.
