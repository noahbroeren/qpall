## What is ISPM 15?

ISPM 15 stands for *International Standards for Phytosanitary Measures No. 15*. The standard was drawn up by the [IPPC](https://www.ippc.int/en/publications/640/) and is designed to stop insects and plant diseases from travelling around the world in wooden packaging. Wooden pallets, crates and dunnage must therefore be treated, usually with heat, and bear an official stamp.

## Why are plastic pallets exempt?

ISPM 15 only applies to packaging material made of solid wood. Plastic is not a natural material in which insects or fungi can settle. A plastic pallet therefore needs no treatment and no stamp, wherever you export to.

## What are the benefits?

- **No treatment costs** and no hassle with certificates.
- **No delays at customs** due to rejected or incorrectly stamped pallets.
- **No risk of the shipment being returned** or destroyed.
- **Always a clean, dry pallet**, free from mould and insects.

## The best plastic pallets for export

For export, light [nestable export pallets](@type-export) are usually the best choice. They weigh little, which saves on freight costs, and they carry up to 2,500 kg static. Choose the size that suits your container and your destination: [1200 x 800](@size-1200x800) for Europe, [1200 x 1000](@size-1200x1000) for the UK, [1100 x 1100](@size-1100x1100) for Asia or the 1220 x 1020 mm pallets for the US.
