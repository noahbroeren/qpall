> **In short**
>
> * The Stack-Up Pallet is a plastic pallet for construction that Q-Pall developed together with its partner Kwakman Afbouw.
> * Each pallet carries forty plasterboards and is hoisted to the right floor during the shell construction phase.
> * Result: 70% less walking, less physical strain and a cleaner building site.
> * Nestable: five to six times as many pallets fit in a return load.
> * In June 2026 the launch was planned for the first quarter of 2027.

## Why the building site needed a better pallet

Wooden pallets have long been the standard in construction. Not because they are the best choice, but because nothing better was widely available. They are heavy, hard to stack efficiently and create a lot of extra handling on site. Read more about the differences in [plastic or wooden pallets](@guide-wood).

For the Dutch interior finishing company Kwakman Afbouw, that was a problem that had to be solved. Together with Q-Pall, the company developed a pallet that fits the way buildings are built today.

## How the Stack-Up Pallet works

* **Forty plasterboards per pallet.** Each Stack-Up Pallet holds forty plasterboards.
* **In place during the shell phase.** The pallets are hoisted to their destination while the shell is being built. The materials are already in the right place before the finishing crews start.
* **Ergonomic height.** The pallet brings the boards to a comfortable working height for the fitters.
* **Nestable.** Empty pallets slide into each other, just like our [nestable pallets](@type-nestable). Five to six times as many pallets fit in a return load. That saves transport costs and labour.

The result: **70% less walking** on the building site, less physical strain for the fitters and a cleaner, faster workflow.

## Circular design

We make our standard pallets from nearly 100% recycled plastic. The Stack-Up Pallet can be dismantled and is built for several projects in a row. At the end of its life, it becomes raw material for new pallets again. That is in line with our approach to [sustainability](@about-sustainability).

## Pilots and launch

By June 2026 the first pilots had been received positively. At that time, the launch of the Stack-Up Pallet was planned for the first quarter of 2027.

The Stack-Up Pallet is a project we developed together with a partner. For other customers, we adapt existing models, for example with their own colour, a logo or a marking. Read more about [customisation](@about-custom).
