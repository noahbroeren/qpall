## Made for sea containers and the chemical industry

The 760 x 1140 mm size is made for efficient container transport. Up to 31 pallets fit in a 40ft container. The size matches the CP5 pallet that the chemical industry uses for bagged goods and boxes. We also see it in the food and pharmaceutical sectors and in international export flows.

## Light, nestable and export-ready

All models in this size are light pallets made from recycled plastic. They carry 2,500 kg static and 1,000 kg dynamic. You can choose from:

- **9 feet, open or closed deck:** nestable, lightweight (from 4.6 kg) and ideal for one-way export.
- **3 runners, open or closed deck:** more stable on roller conveyors and when stacking loaded pallets.

A plastic pallet is exempt from [ISPM 15](@guide-ispm). So you do not need to arrange heat treatment or a stamp. When empty, the pallets with feet slide into each other, so returns or storage take up little space.
