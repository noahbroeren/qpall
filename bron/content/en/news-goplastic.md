## Ten years together

Goplasticpallets.com (All Pallets Ltd, Eastbourne) sells Q-Pall plastic pallets in the United Kingdom and is our partner and [dealer](@dealers) there. In 2022 the partnership turned ten.

To mark the occasion, Managing Director Jim Hardisty looked back. What makes the partnership special, which pallets do well on the UK market and what does he expect from the future?

## Quick answers, even to technical questions

"As I look back on the last ten years working with Q-Pall, it gives me a great sense of fulfilment to see how far we've both come," said Hardisty. "They have never stopped moving. They have a strong focus on innovation and are always researching new products to serve both existing and emerging markets."

According to him, the Q-Pall team was fairly small but extremely efficient and responsive. "We pride ourselves on being the UK's plastic pallet experts, but we don't always hold the answers to our customers' sometimes very technical questions. Then we can rely on Q-Pall. They're always quick to pick up the phone and reply to our emails."

## Stock and production

Goplasticpallets.com fulfils orders direct from stock. That means its manufacturing partner has to respond fast to large quantities. "With access to four manufacturing sites, Q-Pall is well equipped to meet our needs and has never let us down."

In 2022 those four sites were in the Netherlands, Denmark and two in Germany. Today we produce in [six factories across Europe](@about-local), including Chesterfield in the United Kingdom. This means we can also produce in the UK itself, close to British customers.

## The pallets UK customers choose

Goplasticpallets.com offered the complete Q-Pall range. The core of the partnership was the range of [medium](@class-medium) and [heavy duty](@class-heavy) pallets.

The company also sold all [nestable pallets](@type-nestable). These are popular with retail customers for point of sale and display, because they are light and easy to handle.

## A personal connection

Hardisty also mentioned the good personal relationship with the Q-Pall team, in particular with Arno Putters and Bjorn Oosterhof, co-founder and director of Q-Pall. He had met most of the team in person on visits to our head office in Veghel.

"When we speak, it's not all business. We exchange news about our families and they very kindly text us on our birthdays. Doing business is much easier when you're sharing it with genuinely nice people."

## Looking ahead

"Looking ahead, we see a long and fruitful partnership," Hardisty said in 2022. "Q-Pall will continue to develop its range to meet the changing demands of the logistics and manufacturing industries. We will continue to expand our stock to meet the evolving needs of our customers. These first ten years have been both enjoyable and rewarding, and we look forward to sharing the next ten."
