## The pallet pool of the Dutch beverage industry

Drankenpallet Beheer Nederland (DPB) is the pallet pool of the Dutch beverage industry. Almost all Dutch breweries and a large number of soft drink and canned food manufacturers rent their pallets from DPB.

On 24 May 2023, Q-Pall ceremonially delivered the first pallet for DPB at its factory in Balk. Up to 2034, we will make at least one million recycled plastic pallets there. It was the largest Dutch order for Q-Pall since it was founded in 2005. Read more about our [plastic pallets for the beverage industry](@sector-beverage) and for [pooling](@sector-pooling).

## Circular ambitions

For DPB, quality, speed and the use of recycled material were what counted. Christiaan Hamminga, director of Drankenpallet Beheer Nederland, said at the delivery:

> "We are very pleased with the collaboration. Q-Pall is constantly looking for ways to improve its products, has a very modern production site with an excellent quality service and short lines of communication. Thanks to these short lines and production in the Netherlands, we can act quickly. This ensures high-quality pallets that are also made from recycled material and therefore contribute to our circular ambitions."

## Robust, lightweight, strong and sustainable

Bjorn Oosterhof, co-founder and director of Q-Pall, was proud of the order:

> "We are incredibly proud that, as a Dutch company, we have been trusted to develop and make the pallet for almost the entire Dutch beverage industry. Thanks to joint development with DPB, we have created a robust pallet that is lightweight yet strong and sustainable. Extensive research and development preceded this. Moreover, the pallets are made from nearly 100% so-called secondary raw materials, such as old crates, caps and other plastic waste. In this way, we make a direct contribution to tackling the global plastic problem. Truly circular."

## Produced locally

The pallets for DPB come from our factory in Balk, in the province of Friesland. That fits the way Q-Pall works: we buy recycled plastic as close to our factories as possible and produce the pallets as close to the customer as possible. Transport is the largest source of CO₂ emissions in the pallet chain, so every kilometre we save counts. Read more about [local production](@about-local).

## About Q-Pall

Since 2005, Q-Pall has made only plastic pallets and is part of the AVK Group. The head office is in Veghel. We produce in six factories in Europe: Balk, Ribe, Marktoberdorf, Nantes, Lleida and Chesterfield. The range comprises around 130 models in nine sizes. We also work with 18 dealers in 15 countries in Europe and the United States.
