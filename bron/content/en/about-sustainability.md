## Recycled plastic as raw material

We make our standard pallets from nearly 100% recycled plastic, a mix of HDPE and PP (light pallets also contain LDPE). This material comes from waste streams such as crates, caps and packaging. Working with recycled plastic takes a lot of expertise, because it behaves differently from new (virgin) plastic. We have built up that expertise since 2005. What HDPE with code 2 is exactly, you can read in [recycled PE-HD 2: what is it exactly?](@news-hdpe) The result: pallets that do what they need to do, with fixed [load ratings](@about-load) for every model.

Only our [hygienic pallets](@type-hygienic) are made of virgin plastic, for applications with direct food contact. All other Q-Pall pallets are made of nearly 100% recycled plastic.

## Your own plastic waste as raw material

We also look at our customers' plastic waste streams. Where possible, we process collected plastic into raw material for new pallets. This way, we close the loop together. [Contact](@contact) us if you want to know what is possible with your waste stream.

## Less transport, less CO₂

In the pallet industry, most CO₂ emissions come from transport. A truck full of pallets mainly carries air. That is why we produce [locally](@about-local), in six factories spread across Europe. Almost every customer is within 600 kilometres of a Q-Pall factory. Plastic pallets are also lighter than many wooden pallets. A lower tare weight means more load per trip and less fuel.

## Built to last, then raw material again

A plastic pallet lasts for many years, much longer than an average wooden pallet. If a pallet does break, it is fully recyclable. The material is ground down and reused for new products. Used pallets in good condition get a second life through [Q-Used](@used).

## EcoVadis Bronze

Our sustainability performance is independently assessed by EcoVadis. We received the silver medal in April 2023 and the bronze medal in 2025. In October 2026, we again achieved the EcoVadis Bronze medal, placing us in the top 35% of assessed companies.

## Taking back your pallets

Have your Q-Pall pallets reached the end of their life, or are they damaged? Then we take them back. We grind them into raw material for new pallets, so the plastic stays in the loop. How the take-back works and what it involves depends on the number of pallets and where they are. [Contact us](@contact) and we will discuss the options.
