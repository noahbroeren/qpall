> **In short**
>
> * Wooden pallets are rough, can splinter and absorb moisture. That is not what you want in hospitality.
> * Plastic pallets are flat and smooth, do not absorb moisture and are easy to clean.
> * Does food come into direct contact with the pallet, or do you work to HACCP? Then choose a hygienic pallet made of virgin plastic.
> * With pallet collars and a lid, you turn a pallet into a closed box.
> * Freezer spacers between the layers help your load freeze faster and more evenly.

Pallets for the hospitality industry have a lot to cope with: moisture, cold, spilled liquids and frequent cleaning. Food, drinks and other supplies are transported and stored in warehouses, cold rooms and freezers. Below you can read why plastic pallets are a good fit and which accessories make the work easier.

## Plastic or wooden pallets in hospitality?

Traditionally, pallets are made of wood. In hospitality, a wooden pallet is not ideal: it is rough and splinters can break off. Wood is also porous by nature. Moisture, dirt and bacteria easily stay behind in small joints and cracks, and insects and other pests can settle in them.

Plastic pallets do not have these drawbacks. The surface is flat and smooth, so dirt cannot settle into the material. Plastic does not absorb moisture, is easy to clean and contains no nails.

## Cleaning pallets after a spill

In hospitality, things sometimes leak: a bottle, a bucket or a container of sauce. Pallets used for transport and storage can then get quite dirty. A plastic pallet is easy to clean because the material is not porous.

Do you also use a drip tray? It catches leaking liquids before they soak the rest of the load. That saves valuable cleaning time.

## Hygienic pallets for food contact and HACCP

We make our standard pallets from nearly 100% recycled plastic. They are intended for packaged products, not for direct contact with food. Does food come into direct contact with the pallet, or do you work in a HACCP environment with strict requirements? Then choose a [hygienic pallet](@type-hygienic). It is made of virgin (new) plastic and has a completely smooth finish, so you can clean it quickly and thoroughly.

## From pallet to closed box

Plastic pallets combine well with [pallet collars](@acc-collars). By stacking several collars on top of each other, you create a box and decide the height yourself. Add a plastic lid and you have a closed box. Dust and dirt stay out, and fragile goods are better protected against damage.

## Freezer spacers for the freezer room

Frozen products are common in hospitality. [Freezer spacers](@acc-freezer) are a useful addition to plastic pallets. They are made for freezing temperatures and carry the weight of the frozen goods.

You place the freezer spacers between the layers of boxes. Thanks to the raised sections with openings, cold air flows between the goods. The whole load freezes quickly and evenly, even when you store a lot at once.

## Keeping drinks stable on the pallet

Do you regularly transport or store drinks in crates, trays or bottles? On a smooth surface, such a load can start to slide. A pallet with an [anti-slip deck](@type-antislip) keeps the load in place better, so things are less likely to fall over.
