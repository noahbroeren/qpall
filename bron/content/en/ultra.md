## Made for the toughest cycles

The Ultra pallet was developed together with major brands from the beverage industry, such as Coca-Cola, Heineken, Bavaria, Vrumona, Pepsi, Grolsch and AB InBev. The result is a pallet that lasts for years in pools, in high racking and in automated warehouses.

## Features

- **Size:** 1200 x 1000 x 165 mm.
- **Anti-slip** on the top and bottom, so the load and pallet do not shift.
- **Closed runners**, available with 3 or 6 runners.
- **Racking load up to 1,375 kg** without metal reinforcements.
- **Impact- and shock-resistant**, suitable for automated conveyor systems.
- **Circular:** made from nearly 100% recycled plastic, including old crates and caps, and fully recyclable.

## Applications

Heavy-duty logistics, open and closed pools, the [beverage industry](@sector-beverage) and automated warehouses. Also see our other [anti-slip pallets](@type-antislip) or all pallets of [1200 x 1000 mm](@size-1200x1000).
