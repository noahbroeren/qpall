## How does nesting work?

On a nestable pallet, the feet are hollow and tapered. Empty pallets therefore fit into each other, like plastic cups. A stack of empty pallets is much lower than a stack of standard pallets. That has three major benefits:

- **Less storage space** for your stock of empty pallets.
- **More pallets per truck** for inbound and return transport.
- **Lower transport costs and less CO₂** per pallet.

## Light and ergonomic

Nestable pallets are the lightest in our range. A nestable 1200 x 800 mm Euro pallet weighs just over 5 kg. That makes them easy to handle, better for your staff and cheaper for air freight. Despite their low weight, they carry up to 2,500 kg static and 1,000 kg dynamic.

## Sizes

We have nestable pallets in 1200 x 800, 1200 x 1000, 1100 x 1100, 1140 x 1140, 1200 x 1200 and 760 x 1140 mm, and the [display pallets](@type-display) of 600 x 400 and 600 x 800 mm. Most are available with an open deck, some also with a closed deck.
