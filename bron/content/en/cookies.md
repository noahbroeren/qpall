## Necessary

This website uses functional storage in your browser (localStorage). It remembers your cookie choice and which pallets you want to compare. This data stays on your own device. We use Cookiebot to record your consent; it sets a necessary cookie for this purpose.

## External services

Some parts are loaded from an external service. These services may set their own cookies. We only load them once you have given your consent, via the cookie notice or with the button on the page itself.

| Service | Where | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| SimplyBook | [Register a transport](@transport) | Booking a time slot for loading or unloading |
| Mach3 Locator | [International dealers](@dealers) | Interactive dealer map |

## Statistics and marketing

With your consent we use the following services on qpall.com. We load them via Google Tag Manager.

| Service | Category | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Google Analytics | Statistics | Insight into how visitors use the website, such as pages viewed and enquiries sent |
| Google Ads | Marketing | Measuring which adverts lead to an enquiry |
| LinkedIn Insight Tag | Marketing | Measuring campaigns on LinkedIn |
| Leadinfo | Marketing | Recognising which companies visit the website |
| HubSpot | Marketing | Linking website visits to your enquiry so that we can follow it up properly |

If you do not give your consent, these services set no cookies. The website still works as normal.

These services are also subject to their own privacy and cookie terms.

## Legal basis

We use necessary storage on the basis of our legitimate interest: a website that works properly. Cookies for statistics and marketing are only placed with your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). We have a data processing agreement with Google.

## Changing your choice

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time via **Cookie settings** at the bottom of every page. You can delete stored data via your browser settings. Read more about how we handle personal data in our [privacy statement](@privacy).
