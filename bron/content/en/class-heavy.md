## Made for intensive use

Heavy pallets have a heavier construction with reinforced runners and a stiffer deck. They are made for the toughest conditions: high racking, heavy loads and many years of use with forklifts, pallet trucks and automated systems.

## Versions

- **Open or closed deck**, on 3, 5 or 6 runners.
- **[Hygienic and cleanroom pallets](@type-hygienic)** for the food and pharmaceutical industries.
- **[CP pallets](@type-cp)** for the chemical industry.
- **The [Ultra pallet](@ultra)**, a heavy-duty pool pallet with anti-slip on top and bottom.
- **[Block pallets](@type-block)** of 1200 x 1000 mm.

## Choosing the right racking load

For pallet racking, the racking load is the most important value. It applies when the pallet rests only on the two beams. Always choose a model with a racking load above the weight of your load. You can read more about this on the page about [pallet load capacity](@about-load).
