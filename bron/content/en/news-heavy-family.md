## A range for heavy use

In 2021 Q-Pall expanded its range of [heavy-duty plastic pallets](@class-heavy) with a complete line of four models. We presented the pallets in September of that year at two trade fairs: SITL in Paris and Fachpack in Nuremberg.

The pallets are made for heavy loads and intensive use. They have a robust design, are impact-resistant and carry up to 1,375 kg in high pallet racking. You can read exactly what racking load means on our page about [the load capacity of plastic pallets](@about-load).

## Versions

The four models differ in size. You can also choose from these versions:

* **3 or 6 runners.** Besides the version with three runners, there is a version with six runners. It is also suitable for block stacking.
* **Open or closed deck.** An open deck lets moisture drain away. A closed deck gives a flat surface for small products and is easy to clean.

## Easy to identify in your supply chain

You can make the heavy-duty pallets easy to recognise with:

* **a custom colour**, for example to distinguish pools or departments;
* **a customer marking**, such as a logo;
* **an RFID tag**, so you can identify and track your pallets.

Read more about logos, colours and other options on our page about [custom pallets](@about-custom).
