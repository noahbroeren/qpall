Besides the 3 runners version, the pallets are available with 6 runners and can therefore be used for block stacking. The pallets come with an open and closed upper deck. Capable of satisfying every specific requirement, the pallets are available with or without rim. The identification of the plastic pallets can be facilitated by selecting different colors and by applying the client's specific mark.

The new line of heavy duty pallets will be introduced during the SITL Paris 2021 and Fachpack 2021 in the month of September.
