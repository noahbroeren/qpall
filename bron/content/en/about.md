## Our mission

From the start, our mission has been clear: to supply cost-effective and reliable plastic pallets, with sustainability as the starting point. Because we focus entirely on the development and production of plastic pallets, we can keep offering new solutions that meet the changing needs of our customers.

Our pallets are made from almost 100% recycled plastic. They are environmentally friendly, without compromising on strength or service life. This way, we reduce waste and at the same time supply pallets you can build on. We call that **pallets with a purpose**.

## Q-Pall in figures

- **Founded in 2005**, with its head office in Veghel.
- **Six production sites** in the Netherlands, Denmark, Germany, France, Spain and the United Kingdom.
- **4 million pallets per year** of production capacity, in around 130 models and nine sizes.
- **200+ people in our production chain.**
- **A dealer network** covering a large part of Europe and the United States.
- **EcoVadis Bronze** (October 2026) for our sustainability performance.

## Act global, produce local

Transport is by far the largest source of CO2 emissions in the pallet chain. That is why we produce as close to our customers as possible: in Balk (NL), Ribe (DK), Marktoberdorf (DE), Nantes (FR), Lleida (ES) and Chesterfield (UK). Almost every customer is within 600 kilometres of a Q-Pall factory. Read more about [local production](@about-local).

## The whole chain in our own hands

Q-Pall handles the design, production, distribution and sale of plastic pallets. This lets us respond quickly, for example with [customisation within existing models](@about-custom), and keep quality under our own control. Our engineers work closely with customers in the chemical, food, pharmaceutical, beverage, retail and logistics sectors.

Since 2020, Q-Pall has been part of the [AVK Group](https://www.avkvalves.com/), an international industrial group headquartered in Denmark.
