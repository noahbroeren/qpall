Choosing plastic pallets is already a sustainable choice. We make our standard pallets from nearly 100% recycled plastic, including material from caps and crates. If you want to go one step further, choose used plastic pallets.

With [Q-Used](@used), we buy used pallets, inspect them and sell them again. They are up to 40% cheaper than new pallets. What is more, they need no new raw material and no energy for production.

Which types of used plastic pallets are the most common? These are the three main ones.

## 1. Multi-use pallets

Multi-use pallets are also called return pallets or pool pallets. They are designed to go round many times, for example in a closed loop or in a [pallet pool](@sector-pooling). That is why they are strong and dimensionally stable, and why they last for many years.

A multi-use pallet costs more to buy than a one-way pallet. Thanks to its long life and extra strength, it is still often the most cost-effective choice per trip. At the end of its life, it is fully recyclable.

Multi-use pallets come in many versions. As a result, they are also regularly available as used pallets.

## 2. CP pallets

CP stands for Chemical Pallet. The [CP pallets](@type-cp) were originally developed to standardise the range of pallets in the European chemical and plastics industry. There are nine types, from CP1 to CP9.

Today, CP pallets are also used in many other industries. They are suitable for drums of liquids, bagged goods, octabins and big bags. Big bags are mainly used to transport raw materials such as powders, pellets and granulate.

CP pallets also regularly become available as used pallets, in various sizes.

## 3. Hygienic pallets

[Hygienic pallets](@type-hygienic) are also known as food pallets. You use them where hygiene comes first, such as in the food and pharmaceutical industries (HACCP). Think of companies that prepare, process, pack, transport or distribute food.

A hygienic pallet has a smooth design that is as closed as possible, without seams and corners where dirt can build up. It does not absorb moisture and is easy to clean. Wood is porous and can go mouldy, which makes it less suitable for these environments.

Our hygienic pallets are made of virgin (new) plastic, in their own colour. Pallets made of recycled plastic are not intended for direct contact with food. Hygienic pallets are sometimes also available as used pallets.
