## Exporting without ISPM 15

Under [ISPM 15](@guide-ispm), wooden pallets for export must be heat-treated and carry a stamp. This prevents insects and diseases from crossing borders with wood packaging. Plastic pallets are exempt from this rule. So you do not need to arrange treatment, no certificates are needed and your shipment is not delayed at customs because of the pallet.

## Light, nestable and strong

An export pallet is usually used only once. So it must be light and affordable, but strong enough for the whole journey. Our export pallets:

- **weigh little**, from 2.7 kg for a half Euro pallet to just over 10 kg for a 1200 x 1000 pallet with closed deck and runners. That saves on freight costs, especially for air freight.
- **are often nestable.** The models with 9 feet slide into each other when empty. You save a lot of space in storage and inbound transport.
- **carry up to 2,500 kg static and 1,000 kg dynamic**, enough for most export loads.
- **are dimensionally stable and clean:** no splinters, nails or moisture in your load.

## 9 feet or 3 runners?

A pallet with **9 feet** is the lightest and nestable, and you can pick it up from four sides with a forklift or pallet truck. A pallet with **3 runners** is more stable, especially on roller conveyors and when stacking loaded pallets. Do you want both? Then click [separate runners](@acc-runners) onto a nestable pallet.
