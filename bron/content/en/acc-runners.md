## Click-on runners: low or high

The click-on runners are 1170 mm long and 100 mm wide. There is a low version of 15 mm and a high version of 30 mm. They click onto a pallet with 9 feet without tools. This gives the pallet 3 continuous runners.

## Standard runners for square pallets

For the nestable pallets of [1100 x 1100](@size-1100x1100), [1140 x 1140](@size-1140x1140) and [1200 x 1200 mm](@size-1200x1200), there are standard runners that are 125 mm wide and 18 mm high, in the length of the pallet.

See all [nestable pallets](@type-nestable) that these runners fit under.
