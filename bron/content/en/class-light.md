## Lightweight, nestable and export-ready

Light pallets are designed to use as little material as possible while still being strong enough for transport. That makes them affordable and lightweight. A nestable Euro pallet weighs just over 5 kg. The models with 9 feet slide into each other when empty. The models with 3 runners are more stable on roller conveyors.

## Typical applications

- **[Export](@type-export):** plastic is exempt from ISPM 15, so you do not need to have anything treated.
- **Air freight:** every kilo saved counts.
- **One-off shipments and seasonal peaks:** affordable and compact to store.
- **Floor storage:** you can stack loaded pallets on the floor within the static load.

## Extra stability with runners

You can click [runners](@acc-runners) onto the nestable pallets with 9 feet. This gives you a stable pallet with 3 runners at the destination, while the pallets can be delivered nested.
