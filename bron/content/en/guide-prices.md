## What determines the price of a plastic pallet?

### 1. Weight and material

The biggest cost item is raw material. A heavy pallet of 25 kg contains five times as much plastic as a light pallet of 5 kg. Hence the price difference. Virgin plastic for hygienic applications is more expensive than recycled plastic.

### 2. Design

A closed deck uses more material than an open deck. More runners make the pallet more stable, but also heavier. Options such as anti-slip, a logo or your own colour also add to the price.

### 3. Quantities and delivery

With larger quantities and full truckloads, the cost per pallet goes down. The packing method also plays a role, for example waterproof-wrapped or strapped stacks.

### 4. Distance to the factory

Transport is a significant part of the cost. Q-Pall produces in [six European factories](@about-local), so the distance to you is usually small. Each product shows the locations where it is made.

## Calculate the cost per trip

A wooden pallet seems cheap. But if it breaks after a few trips, leaves splinters in your load or first has to be treated for export, the sums work out differently. A plastic pallet lasts for many years. Divide the price by the number of trips and you see the real cost. Looking for a lower entry price? View our [used plastic pallets](@used).
