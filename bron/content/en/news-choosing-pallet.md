When it comes to choosing a pallet, there are many options. Plastic pallets offer advantages over wood or metal, but there are big differences between plastic pallets too. To choose the right plastic pallet, list the most important factors. Want to get started straight away? Then use our [step-by-step guide to choosing a plastic pallet](@guide-choose).

## Load: how much does the pallet need to carry?

Load capacity is probably the most important factor. How much weight a pallet can take depends on its design and material. Every pallet has three load values:

* **Static.** The pallet stands still on the floor, for example in block stacking.
* **Dynamic.** The pallet is moved with a forklift, pallet truck or roller conveyor.
* **Racking.** The pallet sits in pallet racking without shelves.

Look at the value that matches your use and allow some margin. The stated values apply to an evenly distributed load. Read more about [the load capacity of plastic pallets](@about-load).

Our pallets are divided into three load classes. Light pallets are light and often nestable, for export and one-way use. Medium pallets are the all-rounders for internal transport and light racking loads. Heavy pallets are made for intensive use and pallet racking, and carry up to 7,500 kg static.

## Size: does the pallet suit your goods and transport?

The pallet must be large enough to carry your load properly and transport it safely. Your means of transport and your racking also determine the size: the pallets have to fit in your truck, trailer or container. In Europe, 1200 x 800 mm is the standard. Our range includes around 130 models in nine sizes, from 600 x 400 to 1300 x 1100 mm. Our overview of [pallet sizes](@guide-sizes) lists them all.

## Material and weight

Not every plastic pallet is made of the same material. We make our standard pallets from recycled plastic, a mix of HDPE and PP. Our hygienic pallets, H1 pallets and cleanroom pallets are made of virgin (new) plastic, in their own colour. Pallets made of recycled plastic are not intended for direct food contact.

Plastic pallets are often lighter than wood, although this depends on the type. A light pallet is much lighter than a wooden euro pallet of around 25 kg. A heavy pallet weighs 17 to 28 kg.

## Nestability: saving space

Some plastic pallets are nestable: empty pallets slide into each other. That saves space in storage and on return transport. A full stack holds, for example, 45 [nestable pallets](@type-nestable), compared with 17 medium pallets. Nestable pallets are usually light pallets. Need more stability on site after all? Then you can fit click-on runners underneath.

## Hygiene

Is hygiene important in your application? Then choose a pallet that does not absorb moisture and is easy to clean. Unlike wood, plastic pallets do not splinter or absorb moisture. For direct food contact, or if your production environment has strict requirements, choose a [hygienic pallet](@type-hygienic) or a cleanroom pallet made of virgin plastic with a smooth deck.

## Cost over the entire service life

The price depends on size, version, material and quantity. Plastic pallets often have a slightly higher purchase price than wooden pallets. However, they last longer and need less maintenance. As a result, the total cost in the long run is often lower, as are the costs of damage, repairs and disposing of broken pallets.

## Environment

Sustainability is becoming more and more important for many companies. Our standard pallets are made of almost 100% recycled plastic. At the end of their life, they are fully recyclable: we take them back and grind them into raw material for new pallets.
