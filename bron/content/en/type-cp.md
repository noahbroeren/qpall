## Why plastic in the chemical industry?

In the [chemical industry](@sector-chemical), safety and product quality come first. Plastic CP pallets help with this:

1. **Fewer weak points.** An injection-moulded pallet has no loose boards or joints.
2. **No nails and splinters.** Your bags, drums and big bags stay intact and your product stays clean.
3. **Long service life.** Plastic CP pallets last many years in normal use.
4. **Clean and dimensionally stable.** They do not absorb moisture and have a constant weight. So they work well on palletising lines and roller conveyors.
5. **Circular.** At the end of its service life, the pallet is fully recyclable.

## Medium or heavy?

The **medium** CP pallets are for normal loads and floor storage. The **heavy** CP pallets carry up to 7,500 kg static, up to 2,200 kg dynamic and up to 1,375 kg in racking. Not sure? On our page about [load capacity](@about-load) you can read how to choose the right value.
