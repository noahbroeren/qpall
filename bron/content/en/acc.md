## Everything around the pallet

A pallet rarely stands alone. A pallet collar turns it into a box, a lid keeps dust and moisture out, and freezer spacers help your products freeze faster. For hazardous substances, there are drip trays and gratings. Our accessories are made of plastic or galvanised steel, last a long time and fit the sizes of our pallets exactly.

## Why plastic accessories?

- **Reusable:** plastic lasts for years and does not absorb moisture.
- **Hygienic:** easy to clean, with no splinters or nails.
- **Export-friendly:** like our pallets, exempt from [ISPM 15](@guide-ispm).
- **Matching:** the sizes match our pallets of [1200 x 800](@size-1200x800), [1200 x 1000](@size-1200x1000), [1200 x 1200](@size-1200x1200) and [600 x 800 mm](@size-600x800).
