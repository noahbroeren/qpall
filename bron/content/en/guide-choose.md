## Step 1: determine the size

Start with your load and your process. What size are your boxes, bags or drums? And which sizes fit your racking, trucks and containers? In Europe, [1200 x 800 mm](@size-1200x800) is the standard. In industry and in the United Kingdom, [1200 x 1000 mm](@size-1200x1000) is common. For sea containers, [1100 x 1100](@size-1100x1100) and [760 x 1140 mm](@size-760x1140) are practical. Big bags and drums are best placed on [1140 x 1140](@size-1140x1140) or [1200 x 1200 mm](@size-1200x1200). Not sure? Our overview of [pallet sizes](@guide-sizes) lists them all.

## Step 2: weigh your heaviest load

Every pallet has three load values. Look at the value that matches your use:

- **Static:** storage on the floor and block stacking.
- **Dynamic:** moving with a forklift, pallet truck or roller conveyor.
- **Racking:** storage in pallet racking without shelves.

Always allow some margin and take unevenly distributed loads into account. Read more about [pallet loads](@about-load).

## Step 3: choose the load class

| Class | Typical use | Racking |
|---|---|---|
| [Light](@class-light) | Export, one-way shipments, floor storage | No |
| [Medium](@class-medium) | Internal transport, distribution, light racking storage | Yes, depending on model |
| [Heavy](@class-heavy) | Intensive use, pools, heavy racking storage | Yes, up to 2,000 kg |

## Step 4: open or closed deck

An **open deck** is lighter and cheaper. Moisture drains away and air can circulate, which is useful for cooling or for products that need to dry. A **closed deck** is flat and solid. Small packages cannot fall through and it is easy to clean. That is important in the [food](@sector-food) and [pharmaceutical](@sector-pharma) industries. For extra grip, choose an [anti-slip deck](@type-antislip).

## Step 5: feet or runners

- **9 feet:** light, often nestable and accessible from four sides by forklift and pallet truck. Ideal for [export](@type-export).
- **3 runners:** stable on the floor, on roller conveyors and in pallet racking.
- **5 or 6 runners:** even more support and a more even load distribution. With 6 runners (perimeter base), the underside is closed all round.

Want a nestable pallet that becomes more stable on site? Then use [click-on runners](@acc-runners).

## Step 6: think about the entire service life

Will the pallet come back? Then a heavier pallet that lasts many trips pays off. Is it a one-way trip? Then a light [nestable pallet](@type-nestable) is the most economical. Also consider extras: a logo or your own colour (see [custom solutions](@about-custom)), [pallet collars](@acc-collars) and [lids](@acc-lids), or [used pallets](@used) if budget matters.
