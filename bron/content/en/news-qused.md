## What is Q-Used?

Q-Used is the label under which Q-Pall offers [used plastic pallets for sale](@used). We introduced it in early 2022. The idea is simple: a pallet that a customer takes out of circulation is often still perfectly usable.

We buy these pallets, inspect them and sell them again. This gives a pallet a second, third or fourth life, without any new raw material. Only when a pallet has really reached the end of its life do we recycle it.

## Why buy used plastic pallets?

Plastic pallets last much longer than [wooden pallets](@guide-wood). That is why a used plastic pallet is often still in good condition. These are the benefits:

* **Cost-effective.** A used pallet is up to 40% cheaper than a new one. The price depends on the type, the quality and availability.
* **Inspected.** Before we sell a pallet, we check it for damage and check its construction.
* **Dimensionally stable.** Plastic does not absorb moisture, so it does not warp.
* **Export-ready.** Like new plastic pallets, used pallets are exempt from [ISPM 15](@guide-ispm).
* **Sustainable.** A used pallet needs no new raw material and no energy for production.

## How it works

Q-Used does not have a webshop. Stock changes all the time, depending on which pallets come in. This is how it works in practice:

1. You tell us the size, the load and the number of pallets you are looking for.
2. We check what is in stock and send you a quote.
3. The pallets are delivered from our warehouse in Veghel.

## Reuse first, then recycle

Every year, more than 300 million tonnes of plastic are produced worldwide. Part of it ends up in the environment and in the sea. The longer a plastic product stays in use, the less new plastic is needed.

That is why we follow this order: first reuse for as long as possible, then recycle. At the end of their life, we grind the pallets into raw material for new pallets. Read more about [recycling plastic pallets](@news-recycling).

We also make our own standard pallets from nearly 100% recycled plastic. That is how we close the loop. You can read more about our approach on our [sustainability](@about-sustainability) page.
