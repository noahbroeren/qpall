## No more repacking

With a display pallet, your product goes from the distribution centre to the shop floor in one go. The pallet is loaded and placed directly in the shop as a display. That saves work in the shop, prevents damage and gives a tidy presentation. Display pallets are used in supermarkets, DIY stores, at trade fairs and for promotions.

## Light, clean and nestable

- **Light:** from 2 kg for the 600 x 400 mm to 5.9 kg for the heavy 600 x 800 mm.
- **Nestable:** empty pallets slide into each other, so return transport takes up little space.
- **Clean:** no splinters, nails or moisture, and easy to clean. Important in [retail](@sector-retail) and for food.
- **Sustainable:** made from nearly 100% recycled plastic and fully recyclable.

- [600 x 800 mm](@size-600x800): the half Euro pallet, in light, medium and heavy.
