## Why anti-slip?

In fast order picking and transport processes, a load shifts easily, especially with smooth boxes, trays or bags. An anti-slip deck provides more grip between pallet and load. That gives you four benefits:

- **More safety:** less risk of shifting or falling loads.
- **Less stretch film:** the load is more stable, so you need fewer wraps.
- **Higher line speed:** pallets can move faster over roller conveyors and through bends.
- **Suitable for AGVs and roller conveyors:** the closed runners run smoothly over conveyor systems.

## The new medium anti-slip pallets

The new generation of medium pallets has a closed top deck with integrated anti-slip strips. They are available in [1200 x 800 mm](@size-1200x800) on 3 runners and in [1200 x 1000 mm](@size-1200x1000) on 3 or 5 runners. They carry 6,000 kg static, 1,500 kg dynamic and 750 to 1,000 kg in racking.

## The Ultra pallet: heavy-duty with anti-slip

For the heaviest applications, there is the [Ultra pallet](@ultra) of 1200 x 1000 x 165 mm. It has anti-slip on top and bottom and carries up to 1,375 kg in pallet racking. The Ultra pallet was developed together with major beverage brands and is made for pools and automated warehouses.
