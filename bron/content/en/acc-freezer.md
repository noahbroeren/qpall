## How does a freezer spacer work?

When you freeze palletised products, the outer boxes cool down much faster than the boxes in the centre. Freezer spacers between the layers create channels through which cold air flows. As a result, the entire load freezes faster and more evenly. That is why spacers are widely used in blast freezers and cold stores for meat, fish, bakery products and ready meals.

## Plastic instead of wood or cardboard

Cardboard goes limp from condensation. Wood absorbs moisture and is difficult to clean. A plastic spacer does not have these drawbacks: you clean it and use it again and again. It is splinter-free and suitable for rotation systems with many cycles.
