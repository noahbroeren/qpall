> **In short**
>
> * Plastic does not absorb moisture. You can clean plastic pallets with water, under high pressure and with detergents.
> * The smoother the pallet, the easier it is to clean. Hygienic pallets have as few seams and corners as possible.
> * Clean small numbers yourself. Large numbers can be outsourced to a specialist pallet washing company.
> * Working to HACCP? Then record the cleaning procedure in your hygiene plan.
> * Ask us about the maximum temperature and suitable agents for your pallet model.

**Cleaning plastic pallets** can seem a tricky job. Some pallets have corners and seams that are hard to reach. The big advantage of plastic is that it can take a knock, including a thorough clean.

How you go about cleaning depends on the type of pallet, the number of pallets and the requirements in your sector.

## Material and shape

The fewer bumps, gaps, edges and corners a pallet has, the easier it is to clean. A pallet with a smooth, closed deck is therefore quicker to clean than one with many openings. Which pallet you use depends on the application.

All Q-Pall [plastic pallets](@pallets) can be cleaned. Plastic is not porous: water and detergent do not soak into the material. The pallet dries quickly and no moisture stays behind.

Our [hygienic pallets](@type-hygienic) have an extra careful finish. They have as few spaces and seams as possible where dirt can build up. These pallets are made of virgin plastic and are intended for processes with strict hygiene requirements.

## Pallet cleaning procedure in eight steps

Whether you clean in-house or outsource it, a fixed procedure gives you consistent results. A common sequence:

1. **Empty.** Remove the load, film, labels and other residues from the pallet.
2. **Inspect.** Check the pallet for damage. Take pallets with cracks or breaks out of circulation.
3. **Remove dry dirt.** Sweep or blow away loose dirt before you start with water.
4. **Pre-rinse.** Rinse the pallet with water to remove coarse dirt.
5. **Clean.** Clean with a suitable detergent and a brush or high-pressure cleaner. Pay extra attention to corners and seams.
6. **Rinse.** Rinse off all detergent residues with clean water.
7. **Disinfect if needed.** Follow your own hygiene plan and the instructions for the product.
8. **Dry and store.** Let the pallet dry thoroughly so that no water is left standing on it. Then store it clean and dry.

Only use agents and temperatures that are suitable for plastic. Ask us about the maximum temperature and suitable agents for the model you use.

## Cleaning to HACCP standards

Hygiene requirements are high in the food and pharmaceutical industries. When cleaning, always take the HACCP requirements in your sector into account. Record how often you clean the pallets, with which agents and who checks it.

Read more about the role of the pallet in [HACCP pallets](@news-haccp).

### Cleanroom pallets

For cleanrooms and other controlled environments, there are cleanroom pallets. They have a closed, smooth design and are made of virgin plastic. You clean them before they enter the controlled area.

Read more on [plastic pallets for the pharmaceutical industry](@sector-pharma).

## Cleaning plastic pallets yourself

Do you have a small number of pallets with a smooth design? Then cleaning them yourself is a good choice. With water, a detergent and a brush or high-pressure cleaner, the pallets are easy to clean.

Do choose a suitable location. You are working with water and detergents, so the area will get wet. The used water also needs to be drained.

Always use detergents that are not harmful to the environment.

## Having plastic pallets cleaned

You can also outsource cleaning to companies that specialise in washing pallets and crates. They clean and dry your pallets with energy-efficient, high-capacity machines. Such pallet washers often have a pre-wash and a main wash zone and can also disinfect.

A machine cleans many pallets in a short time, with consistent results. Many providers also collect and return the pallets. For large numbers, such as in the [food industry](@sector-food), this is a practical solution.
