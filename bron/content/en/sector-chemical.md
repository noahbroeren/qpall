## The challenge

Chemical products are often transported in bags, big bags, octabins or drums. A nail or splinter can tear open the packaging, resulting in product loss or a dangerous situation. In addition, many chemical companies work with automated lines that require consistent dimensions. And sometimes pallets have to be used in controlled environments.

## Why plastic pallets?

- **Resistant to moisture and chemical substances.** Plastic does not absorb liquid and does not rot.
- **No nails or splinters.** Your packaging stays intact and your product stays clean.
- **Dimensionally stable with a consistent weight.** Ideal for palletising robots and roller conveyors.
- **As strong as wood**, but with a much longer service life.
- **Circular:** made from nearly 100% recycled plastic and fully recyclable at the end of its life.
