## Good to know

- Please book your time slot at least one working day in advance.
- Have your vehicle registration number and the order or reference number to hand.
- Around the holidays some sites have limited staffing. You can see this per site above the calendar.
- Running late or unable to make it? Cancel or move your time slot via the confirmation email, so another driver can use the slot.
