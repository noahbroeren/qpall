## A closed unit load

A lid closes off your pallet or a stack of [pallet collars](@acc-collars). It protects the load against dust, dirt and splash water and gives the stack a neat top. Together with a pallet and one or more collars, the lid forms complete, reusable packaging.

## Plastic instead of cardboard or wood

A plastic lid does not go limp from moisture like cardboard, and it does not absorb moisture like wood. You clean it easily and use it again and again. Plastic packaging is also exempt from [ISPM 15](@guide-ispm).
