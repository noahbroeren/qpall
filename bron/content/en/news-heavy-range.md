> **In short**
>
> * With the QP line, Q-Pall expanded its range of heavy-duty plastic pallets with the QP 1111H, 1140H and 1212H.
> * The pallets carry 7,500 kg static, 2,000 kg dynamic and up to 1,375 kg in racking.
> * Sizes: 1100 x 1100, 1140 x 1140 and 1200 x 1200 mm, all with a height of 165 mm.
> * Thanks to the design and the recycled plastic, no metal reinforcements are needed.
> * Versions are available with three or six runners and with an open or closed deck.

## What makes the QP line different

The QP line combines the advantages of classic [heavy-duty plastic pallets](@class-heavy) with what we have learned about design, materials and production technology. The shape of the pallet follows from what it has to do: carry heavy loads, year after year.

The mould for the QP series has a flexible set-up. With different mould options we make pallets with three or six runners and with an open or closed deck.

## Strong without metal

The QP 1111H, 1140H and 1212H carry 7,500 kg static and 2,000 kg dynamic. In racking they carry up to 1,375 kg; the exact value depends on the model and version. You can read what these values mean on our page about [the load capacity of plastic pallets](@about-load).

No metal reinforcements are needed. That is down to the design and to the high-quality plastic, which is almost 100% recycled. Without metal, the pallets stand up well to tough conditions and are easy to recycle at the end of their life.

## A design for intensive use

The top deck consists of individual 'box' sections that make the deck stiff and strong. Robust corners protect against impacts. Rounded edges on the underside reduce the risk of damage at the fork entries.

As a result, the pallets last a long time and stay impact-resistant, even at high rotation rates such as in open and closed [pools](@sector-pooling).

## Models and options

| Model | Size (mm) | Racking load |
|---|---|---|
| QP 1111H | 1100 x 1100 x 165 | 1,000 to 1,250 kg |
| QP 1140H | 1140 x 1140 x 165 | 1,250 to 1,375 kg |

The racking load depends on the number of runners and on the deck (open or closed).

The standard colour is black. Another colour, a logo or another adjustment is possible; see [custom pallets](@about-custom). On request, the pallets are also available in an [ESD version](@news-esd) and in a flame-retardant version.

## An investment for years

Arno Putters said: "Our plastic pallets have been the favourite choice of many companies since our start in 2005. We continue to see a strong increase in demand, because of their performance and the Q-Pall business philosophy. Plastic pallets are also seen as environmentally friendly, because they can be fully recycled at the end of their life."

Depending on use, a heavy-duty plastic pallet lasts between eight and fifteen years. With intensive use, it therefore pays for itself compared with wooden pallets.
