## The challenge

In the food industry, the requirements for hygiene and food safety are high. Pallets must not be a source of contamination. At the same time, they need a consistent weight and fixed dimensions for automated lines. And in cold stores and freezer rooms, they must remain strong at low temperatures.

## Why plastic pallets?

- **Easy to clean:** smooth surfaces without seams where dirt can build up.
- **Resistant to moisture and chemicals**, so they also withstand intensive cleaning.
- **No splinters or nails** that end up in your product or machines.
- **Long service life** and more economical than wood over the entire service life.
- **Fully recyclable.**

For a full comparison, see [plastic or wooden pallets](@guide-wood).

## Virgin or recycled?

For direct food contact, use a pallet made of virgin plastic, which poses no risk of contamination. For packaged products, where the pallet does not come into direct contact with the food, our other pallets made of recycled plastic are a sustainable choice. Our [hygienic pallets](@type-hygienic) are made of virgin plastic, all other Q-Pall pallets of recycled plastic.

## Open or closed deck?

| | Open deck | Closed deck |
|---|---|---|
| Cleaning | Possible, but dirt stays behind in the openings more easily | Quick and thorough |
| Moisture | Can leak through the deck | Stays on the deck and can be rinsed off |
| Typical use | Packaged products, storage, distribution | Unpackaged products, wet areas, pharma |

## Which pallet for which application?

- **Meat and fish:** hygiene requirements are high, and moisture such as blood or water must not drip onto the load below. Choose a [hygienic pallet](@type-hygienic) with a closed deck.
- **Pharma:** a smooth, closed, non-porous surface that resists cleaning agents and disinfectants, with fixed dimensions for automated lines. See also [the pharmaceutical industry](@sector-pharma).
- **Wet areas**, such as production halls and washing lines: a closed deck that does not absorb water and is quick to clean.
- **Packaged products and distribution:** a standard pallet made of recycled plastic, with an open or closed deck, is sufficient here.

## HACCP

Do you work according to HACCP? Then you want to keep the risk of contamination as low as possible. A pallet that does not absorb moisture, does not go mouldy and is easy to clean helps you do that. Read more in [pallets and HACCP](@news-haccp).

## European rules for food contact

Materials that come into direct contact with food fall under European Regulation (EC) No 1935/2004. They must not transfer substances to food in quantities that could endanger human health. If the pallet comes into direct contact with your product, choose a pallet made of virgin plastic. We are happy to discuss which version suits your application.
