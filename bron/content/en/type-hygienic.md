## For processes where hygiene matters

In the food industry, pharmaceuticals and cleanrooms, a pallet must not be a source of contamination. Wooden pallets absorb moisture, can go mouldy and leave splinters and fibres behind. Plastic pallets do not have these problems. A hygienic pallet goes one step further: it has a smooth, closed design that you can clean quickly and thoroughly.

## The range

- **Hygienic pallets 1200 x 800 mm:** closed or open deck on 3 runners, 7,500 kg static and up to 1,750 kg in racking.
- **H1 Euro pallet:** the well-known pallet from the meat industry, with open deck on 3 runners.
- **Hygienic pallets 1200 x 1000 mm:** closed deck on 3 runners, up to 2,000 kg racking load.
- **Cleanroom pallets:** pallets with closed deck on 3 or 5 runners for cleanrooms and other controlled environments.
- **Industrial pallet 1200 x 1000 mm:** a robust pallet with open deck for intensive industrial use.

## HACCP, GMP and food safety

Companies that work according to HACCP or GMP must control contamination risks. A pallet that you can clean and that does not absorb moisture helps with this. Read more about our approach for the [food industry](@sector-food) and the [pharmaceutical industry](@sector-pharma).
