> **In short**
>
> * Most damage to plastic pallets happens when driving with a forklift or pallet truck.
> * Pay attention to the position, the length and the spacing of the forks.
> * Every pallet has three load values: static, dynamic and racking.
> * These values apply to an evenly distributed load.

Plastic pallets are strong and last for many years. Even so, in practice we keep seeing the same mistakes that cause damage, both to the pallet and to the load. Most of them are easy to prevent, so it pays to go through them with your staff.

## Mistakes when driving the forklift

### 1. Turning before the forks are out of the pallet

Only turn away once the forks are completely out of the pallet. If you turn earlier, the forks hit the inside of the pallet. This can damage the pallet or push it out of place.

### 2. Carrying the pallet too low

If you drive too low, the pallet and its load can hit the ground, for example at a threshold or on uneven ground. Driving up or down a ramp can also quickly go wrong.

### 3. Throwing pallets, leaving them lying around or dragging them

Pallets lying around create dangerous situations and cause accidents. Throwing pallets or needlessly dragging them across the floor also damages them. So stack empty pallets neatly.

### 4. Forks not horizontal

Make sure the forks are horizontal when entering and leaving the pallet, when lifting and when setting it down. This prevents unnecessary damage to the pallets.

### 5. Wrong fork length

If the forks are too long and stick out far at the back, the load behind can also be damaged. If, on the other hand, the load rests too far out on the fork tips, it is likely to tip over. The plastic pallet will then be damaged as well.

### 6. Wrong distance between the forks

The centre distance between the forks also matters for a stable load. It should be about 60% of the width of the pallet.

## Mistakes with load and storage

Every plastic pallet has three load values. They apply to an evenly distributed load, and you will find them for each model on its product page. You can read more on our page about [the load capacity of plastic pallets](@about-load).

### 7. Incorrect use in pallet racking

The racking load is the maximum weight a pallet carries in pallet racking without decking. The pallet then rests only on two beams. This is often the lowest of the three values.

Light pallets are not intended for racking. Choose a Medium or [Heavy pallet](@class-heavy) instead, and place it with its runners at right angles to the beams. We test the racking load with an evenly distributed load of 25 kg bags.

### 8. Misjudging the static load

The static load is the maximum weight a pallet carries when it stands still on a flat floor. When [stacking loaded pallets](@news-stacking), the bottom pallet carries the weight of all the pallets above it. That total must stay within the static load.

### 9. Too heavy a load during transport

The dynamic load is the maximum weight a pallet carries while it is moving, for example on a forklift or pallet truck. Because of acceleration, braking and small shocks, this value is lower than the static load. So pay extra attention to the weight when moving the pallet.

## Preventing damage starts with the right choice

You can prevent a lot of damage simply by choosing a pallet that suits how you use it: on the floor, on the move or in racking. Choose a pallet whose load values are well above the weight of your heaviest load. For help, see [choose the right plastic pallet](@guide-choose).
