## How many pallets fit in a truck?

That depends on the size and on whether the pallets are loaded or empty.

- **Loaded pallets** stand in a single layer on the floor. A standard 13.6 m truck takes 33 Euro pallets (1200 x 800 mm) or 26 pallets of 1200 x 1000 mm.
- **Empty plastic pallets** are stacked or nested. Then many more fit, for example 495 to 598 Euro pallets with runners or 1,485 nestable Euro pallets.

The load calculator works with empty pallets, as Q-Pall delivers them. The table above shows the standard load per size.

## Nestable or stackable

Nestable pallets (light, on 9 feet) slide into each other. That is why almost three times as many fit in a truck as pallets with runners. Pallets with runners or feet are stacked on top of each other, up to the maximum stack height of the model.

## Truck, mega trailer or container

A mega trailer is as long as a standard truck (13.6 m), but 3 m high inside instead of 2.65 m. With stacked pallets, up to just over 20% more fit. A sea container is narrower and lower than a truck. For Euro pallets, a 20 ft container takes about a third of a truckload and a 40 ft high cube container about three quarters. Square pallets of 1200 x 1200 mm fit only one wide in a container, so the difference is bigger there.

## How the load calculator works

1. **Transport.** Choose a standard truck (13.6 m), a mega trailer, a truck with trailer or a 20, 40 or 45 ft sea container.
2. **Pallet.** Choose the size and the version. You can combine several pallet types in one load.
3. **Quantity.** Choose "As many as possible" or enter a fixed number. For nestable pallets, the calculator can also include the loose runners.
4. **Result.** You see how many pallets fit or how many trucks you need, with a detailed loading plan.

## Good to know

- The result is an indication based on standard dimensions.
- The calculator keeps a 0.1 m margin in the length and in the height of the load space. Not in the width: otherwise three Euro pallets would no longer fit side by side.
