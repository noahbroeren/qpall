## All-round use

Medium pallets are the all-rounders in our range. They are sturdier than light pallets, but lighter and more economical than heavy pallets. This makes them ideal for internal transport, distribution, block stacking and light to medium-heavy racking storage.

## Versions

- **Open deck:** light, moisture drains away and air circulates around the load.
- **Closed deck:** flat and easy to clean. Good for small packaging.
- **Anti-slip:** the new generation of medium pallets has an integrated anti-slip deck. See [anti-slip pallets](@type-antislip).
- **3, 5 or 6 runners:** more runners give more support on the floor and on roller conveyors.

## Sizes

Medium pallets are available in [1200 x 800](@size-1200x800), [1200 x 1000](@size-1200x1000), [1100 x 1100](@size-1100x1100), [1140 x 1140](@size-1140x1140) and [1200 x 1200 mm](@size-1200x1200), plus the [display pallets](@type-display) in 600 x 400 and 600 x 800 mm.
