## The quarter Euro pallet

600 x 400 mm is a quarter of a Euro pallet. That makes this size ideal for promotions and displays in retail. The pallet is loaded at the distribution centre and goes straight into the shop on a pallet truck or a dolly. No repacking is needed. That saves time and manual work in the shop.

## Light, sturdy and nestable

The medium display pallet with 4 feet weighs 2 kg and carries 500 kg static and 250 kg dynamic. An open deck lets moisture drain away. When empty, the pallets nest inside each other, with 200 per stack. This keeps storage and return transport compact.
