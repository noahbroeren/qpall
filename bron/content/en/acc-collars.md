## From pallet to transport box

Place the pallet collar on a pallet of the same size and fill it with your goods. Need more height? Then add a second or third collar. A matching [lid](@acc-lids) closes off the load. This way, a set of pallet, collars and lid replaces cardboard boxes and wooden crates in your internal logistics and transport.

## Plastic instead of wood

A plastic pallet collar does not absorb moisture, does not rot and has no splinters or nails. You clean it easily and use it again and again. And like our pallets, it is exempt from [ISPM 15](@guide-ispm), so it is also suitable for export.
