>
> * **The innovation:** medium pallets in 1200 x 800 and 1200 x 1000 mm with a closed top deck and integrated anti-slip strips.
> * **The problem:** shifting loads cause product damage, safety risks and disruptions in automated lines.

## Why load securing is the foundation of logistics

Load securing is one of the most underestimated aspects of logistics. A pallet in motion, whether on a forklift, a roller conveyor or in a truck, must remain stable. When boxes or crates start to slide, problems arise in the chain straight away:

* **Safety risks.** Falling loads can seriously injure employees.
* **Product damage.** Damaged goods cannot be sold, which directly affects your margin.
* **Process disruption.** In automated warehouses, for example with AGVs, a single shifted box can trigger a sensor and stop the entire line.

Anti-slip is not a legal requirement, by the way. Why it is often a sensible choice anyway is explained in [anti-slip pallets: are they mandatory?](@news-antislip-mandatory)

## Two sizes, available as standard

To meet the growing demand for safety and automation, we further developed our medium pallets. The new anti-slip medium pallets have a closed top deck with strips of a rubber-like material (TPE). They are available in two standard sizes:

* **1200 x 800 mm** on 3 runners.
* **1200 x 1000 mm** on 3 or 5 runners.

You will find the load values for each model under [anti-slip pallets](@type-antislip). For heavy applications and pools, there is also the heavy [Ultra pallet](@ultra) of 1200 x 1000 mm, with anti-slip on top and bottom.

## Four benefits for your supply chain

* **More safety.** The anti-slip strips create friction, so the load stays in place. The risk of sliding becomes much smaller, even during internal transport.
* **Less packaging material.** Pallets are often heavily wrapped in stretch film for stability. Because the pallet itself offers more grip, you need less film. That saves costs and plastic waste.
* **Higher throughput.** In distribution centres where speed matters, stable loads make for a smoother process. Roller conveyors can run faster without disruptions.
* **Ready for automation.** Logistics is automating quickly. [AGVs and roller conveyors](@guide-automation) need pallets with fixed dimensions and a stable load. These pallets have been designed for exactly that.

## Sustainable and fully recyclable

At Q-Pall, we believe innovation and circularity should go hand in hand. With traditional anti-slip solutions, rubber strips are often difficult to recycle or have to be removed by hand first.

With our new medium pallets, that is not necessary. The anti-slip strips fit into the same recycling process as the pallet itself. So you do not need to detach them at the end of the pallet's life: the pallet is ground **in its entirety, in one go** into granulate for new Q-Pall pallets. Like our other standard pallets, the pallet itself is made of recycled plastic, a mix of HDPE and PP.
