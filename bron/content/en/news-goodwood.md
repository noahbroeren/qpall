> **In short**
>
> * At the Goodwood Festival of Speed, electric cars were displayed on Q-Pall heavy-duty plastic pallets.
> * The pallet used was the QP1210 HR 5R, with a static load capacity of 7,500 kg.
> * Cars on the pallets included an e.GO, a Kia EV6, a Jaguar and the Rimac Nevera.
> * The pallets were supplied through our UK partner and dealer Goplasticpallets.com.
> * The pallet is made from nearly 100% recycled plastic.

## A podium of plastic pallets

The Goodwood Festival of Speed is one of the best-known events on the motorsport calendar. Our heavy-duty plastic pallets did not race around the hairpins there. Instead they carried the electric cars on show at the festival, demonstrating just how strong they are.

This was made possible by our [UK partner and dealer Goplasticpallets.com](@news-goplastic).

From the e.GO and the Kia EV6 to a Jaguar and the Rimac Nevera: they all took their place on a podium of plastic pallets. The Rimac Nevera stood out for its price tag. Only 150 of these electric hypercars were to be built, at around 2.4 million dollars each.

## Star of the show: the QP1210 HR 5R

The QP1210 HR 5R is a [heavy-duty plastic pallet](@class-heavy) of [1200 x 1000 mm](@size-1200x1000) with a static load capacity of 7,500 kg. That is the load a pallet carries when it stands still on a flat floor. The weight of these cars was therefore no problem for the pallet. You can read more about static, dynamic and racking loads on our page about [the load capacity of plastic pallets](@about-load).

The pallet is designed for use across a wide range of sectors. And because it is made from nearly 100% recycled plastic, it is a responsible choice for your logistics and storage.
