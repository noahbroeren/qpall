## From wooden pallet to smart pallet

The switch from wooden pallets to smart plastic pallets is no longer a concept, but reality. From late 2025, retailer Auchan rolled out 50,000 of our smart [heavy-duty](@class-heavy) Euro pallets made from recycled plastic across France. They are pallets in the Euro pallet size of [1200 x 800 mm](@size-1200x800).

The project contributes to Auchan's goal of cutting scope 3 emissions by 25% by 2030.

## From load carrier to data point

A pallet is no longer just a carrier, but also a source of data. The pallets were developed in collaboration with IBM Consulting and provide real-time insight into the supply chain. This 'Smart Green Pallet' measures, among other things:

* **Location.** Knowing exactly where the goods are.
* **Temperature.** Making sure fresh products stay in the right condition.
* **Shocks.** Seeing where and when goods are mishandled.

In case of temperature fluctuations or hard shocks, the system raises an alarm immediately. This allows teams to step in on time.

## Environmental impact: the figures

In closed systems, plastic pallets have environmental advantages over wood. Auchan's figures confirm this. The switch is estimated to save **21,328 tonnes of CO₂ over eight years**. Two factors make the difference:

* **Service life.** Our pallets last much longer than wooden pallets and are fully recyclable at the end of their life.
* **Less waste.** Monitoring means less food is lost. Food waste accounts for around 5% of logistics emissions.

## A successful pilot

The full roll-out was preceded by a pilot with 10,000 pallets in two warehouses. The results: better tracking, more safety thanks to the lower weight and higher logistics quality.
