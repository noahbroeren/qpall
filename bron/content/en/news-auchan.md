## From wooden pallet to smart pallet

The switch from wooden pallets to smart plastic pallets is no longer a concept, but reality. Retailer Auchan is rolling out 50,000 of our smart [heavy duty](@class-heavy) Euro pallets made from recycled plastic across France. The project contributes to Auchan's goal of cutting scope 3 emissions by 25% by 2030.

## From load carrier to data point

A pallet is no longer just a carrier, but also a source of data. The pallets were developed in collaboration with IBM Consulting and provide real-time insight into the supply chain. This 'Smart Green Pallet' measures, among other things:

* **Location:** knowing exactly where the goods are.
* **Temperature:** making sure fresh products stay in the right condition.
* **Shocks:** seeing where and when goods are mishandled.

In case of temperature fluctuations or hard shocks, the system raises an alarm immediately, so teams can step in on time.

## Environmental impact: the figures

At Q-Pall we have been showing for years that plastic has environmental advantages over wood in closed systems. Auchan's figures confirm this. The switch is estimated to save **21,328 tonnes of CO2 over eight years**. Two factors make the difference:

* **Service life:** our pallets last much longer than wooden pallets and are fully recyclable.
* **Less waste:** monitoring means less food is lost. Food waste accounts for around 5% of logistics emissions.

## A successful pilot

The full roll-out was preceded by a pilot with 10,000 pallets in two warehouses. The results: better tracking, more safety thanks to the lower weight and higher logistics quality.
