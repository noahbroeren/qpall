## Large surface, heavy-duty construction

The 1300 x 1100 mm size offers plenty of space for bulky goods. It matches the CP7 pallet and, rotated, the CP4 pallet used in the chemical industry. You will find it in intralogistics, chemicals, export and distribution, in warehouses with high racking and in block stacking.

## The range

All models are heavy pallets made from recycled plastic, 158 to 160 mm high:

- **Open deck with 3, 5 or 6 runners:** light relative to the load, and moisture drains away.
- **Closed deck with 3 or 6 runners:** a flat, closed surface that is easy to clean.

Every model carries 7,500 kg static, 1,500 kg dynamic and 1,375 kg in racking. Plastic is resistant to moisture and many chemicals, and is exempt from [ISPM 15](@guide-ispm).
