Some products ask more of a pallet than others. Think of chilled products and food that can spoil if hygiene is not up to standard.

With products like these, it is not just the packaging that has to offer protection. How you transport and store them matters too. That is what the **H1 pallet** was made for.

## What is an H1 pallet?

The H1 pallet is a plastic pallet in the size of the Euro pallet: 1200 x 800 mm. The type was originally developed as a pool pallet for the meat industry. Today, the entire food industry uses it.

An H1 pallet is a [hygienic pallet](@type-hygienic). It is smooth and finished to keep seams and corners where dirt can build up to a minimum. Our H1 Euro pallet has an open deck on three runners, so moisture can drain away and air can circulate around the load.

We make H1 pallets from virgin (new) plastic. That sets them apart from our standard pallets, which are made of recycled plastic.

## Hygiene and food safety: HACCP

Companies that handle food must do so safely and hygienically. They are responsible for making sure consumers do not get ill. That is why they work with a food safety plan based on HACCP (Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points).

In such a plan, a company maps out what can go wrong and how to prevent it. This also covers the transport of raw materials and (semi-)finished products, and storage in clean areas.

A pallet that does not absorb moisture and is easy to clean helps keep those risks small. Read more about [pallets and HACCP](@news-haccp).

## Why plastic and not wood?

Wood is porous. It absorbs moisture and gives pests and insects a place to nest. Wooden pallets can also splinter, and splinters are exactly what you do not want near food.

Plastic is not porous and does not splinter. An H1 pallet has no nails and is completely smooth. That leaves few places where dirt can remain, and you can clean the pallet quickly and thoroughly.

Find out how in [cleaning plastic pallets](@news-cleaning).

## When do you need an H1 pallet?

The H1 pallet is used in the food industry. This means companies that prepare, treat, process, package, transport or distribute food. A well-known example is the meat processing industry.

Suppliers of raw materials and processing aids to the food industry also work to HACCP, as do companies that make animal feed. 'Foodstuffs' is a general term for everything people eat and drink.

A rule of thumb for your choice:

* **Does the food come into direct contact with the pallet?** Then choose a pallet made of virgin plastic, such as the H1 pallet or another hygienic pallet.
* **Is the food packaged on the pallet?** Then a pallet made of recycled plastic may be enough. Those pallets are not intended for direct contact with food.
