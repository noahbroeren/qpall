## The challenge

In the pharmaceutical industry, everything revolves around preventing contamination and protecting products during storage and transport. Regulations such as GMP (Good Manufacturing Practices) set high requirements for materials and processes. A wooden pallet is a poor fit: it is porous, can go mouldy and sheds fibres and splinters.

## Why plastic pallets?

- **Non-porous:** the pallets do not absorb moisture and are easy to clean.
- **Washable and cleanable** with water, high pressure or cleaning agents.
- **Splinter-free, with no sharp edges.**
- **Anti-slip available**, for stability on wet or sloping surfaces.
- **Resistant to temperature changes.**
- **Less maintenance and lower replacement costs** than wood.
