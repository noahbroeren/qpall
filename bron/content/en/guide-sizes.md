## The Euro pallet: EUR 1, EUR 2, EUR 3 and EUR 6

The Euro pallet (EUR 1) measures 1200 x 800 mm. A wooden EPAL pallet is 144 mm high and carries up to 1,500 kg. A standard 13.6 metre trailer holds 33 of them. The abbreviations come from the EPAL system for wooden pallets:

- **EUR 1:** 1200 x 800 x 144 mm, the standard Euro pallet.
- **EUR 2:** 1200 x 1000 x 162 mm, an industrial pallet.
- **EUR 3:** 1000 x 1200 x 144 mm, also an industrial pallet, with the height of the Euro pallet.
- **EUR 6:** 800 x 600 x 144 mm, the half Euro pallet.

EUR 2 and EUR 3 share the same footprint, but differ in construction, height and the side from which you pick them up with a forklift. Q-Pall plastic pallets have the same outer dimensions as these standard pallets; height, weight and load capacity differ per model. See the [Euro pallets of 1200 x 800 mm](@size-1200x800) and the [industrial pallets of 1200 x 1000 mm](@size-1200x1000).

## ISO 6780: six international sizes

The international standard ISO 6780 lists six pallet sizes that are widely used around the world:

- **1200 x 800 mm**: the Euro pallet, standard in Europe.
- **1200 x 1000 mm**: the industrial pallet, in Europe, the UK and Asia.
- **1219 x 1016 mm (48 x 40 inch)**: the standard in North America.
- **1165 x 1165 mm**: mainly in Australia.
- **1100 x 1100 mm**: in Asia, including Japan and Korea. See [1100 x 1100 mm](@size-1100x1100).
- **1067 x 1067 mm**: in North America, Europe and Asia.

## Small sizes for retail

The **half Euro pallet** of [600 x 800 mm](@size-600x800) and the **quarter Euro pallet** of [600 x 400 mm](@size-600x400) are popular as display pallets in supermarkets. Two half or four quarter pallets fill exactly one Euro pallet.

## CP sizes for the chemical industry

The chemical industry works with nine CP sizes. The main ones are CP1 and CP6 (1200 x 1000 mm), CP3 and CP9 (1140 x 1140 mm), CP5 (760 x 1140 mm) and CP7 (1300 x 1100 mm). See [CP pallets](@type-cp).

## Which pallet size for which application?

The right size depends on what you transport, how you store it and where the pallet is going. A pallet that is too large wastes space in the racking and the truck; a pallet that is too small does not support the load properly.

### Food

In the food industry, the Euro pallet and the industrial pallet are the standard. You use the half and quarter Euro pallet for displays on the shop floor. If products lie directly on the pallet, choose a [hygienic pallet](@type-hygienic) made of virgin plastic. Read more about [plastic pallets for the food industry](@sector-food).

### Export

For export, choose an international standard size: 1200 x 800 mm within Europe, 1200 x 1000 mm for industry and 1219 x 1016 mm (48 x 40 inch) for North America. For wooden pallets, [ISPM 15](@guide-ispm) usually applies when exporting outside the EU; plastic pallets fall outside it. Lightweight [export pallets](@type-export) keep the freight weight down.

### High-bay warehouses and racking

In a high-bay warehouse, the pallet must match the racking system. Pay attention to:

- **Dimensional accuracy:** every pallet has the same dimensions, even after years of use. This matters for cranes and conveyors.
- **Deflection and racking load:** even a small deformation can cause breakdowns. So besides the static and dynamic load, also look at the load in racking.
- **Two-way or four-way:** you pick up a two-way pallet from two sides, a four-way pallet from all four.
- **Loading height:** for general cargo, 220 cm (pallet plus load) is often the limit, but carriers sometimes use other limits.

More about [plastic pallets for automation](@guide-automation).

### Containers

A 20 ft sea container is about 5.9 metres long inside, a 40 ft container about 12 metres. Euro pallets do not always fill a container well. With industrial pallets of 1200 x 1000 mm, square pallets of [1100 x 1100 mm](@size-1100x1100) or CP5 pallets of [760 x 1140 mm](@size-760x1140), you often make better use of the space.

## Height and fork entry height

Besides length and width, height matters. It determines how many pallets fit in a stack or truck and whether the forks of your forklift or pallet truck fit underneath. Our pallets are 143 to 165 mm high. For each model you can see the exact height and a technical drawing with the dimensions.
