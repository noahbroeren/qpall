> **In short**
>
> * Anti-slip pallets are not a legal requirement.
> * Your load must, however, stand securely on the pallet during transport and storage. You are responsible for that.
> * Anti-slip helps most with smooth packaging, valuable or hazardous loads and fast processes with roller conveyors or AGVs.
> * Q-Pall has anti-slip pallets in its standard range: medium pallets in 1200 x 800 and 1200 x 1000 mm, and the heavy Ultra pallet.

A pallet should make sure goods arrive in one piece. But if a slippery load is placed on it, the load can shift during transport or storage. That causes damage, and with some products, substances can be released that are hazardous to health. An anti-slip pallet helps prevent this. But are you required to use anti-slip pallets?

## Why loads shift on a pallet

Packaged products with a smooth surface, such as smooth boxes, trays, bags or crates, easily slide across the top deck of a pallet. This happens mainly when braking and cornering, when the pallet is moved with a forklift and on roller conveyors. A shifting load leads to:

* **Damage.** Damaged products cannot be sold.
* **Danger.** Falling loads can injure employees. With hazardous substances, damaged packaging can start to leak.
* **Disruption.** In an automated warehouse, a single shifted box can stop an entire line.

## Are anti-slip pallets mandatory?

No. There is no legal obligation to use anti-slip pallets. However, a load must be secure during transport and storage. Anyone who transports goods, or has them transported, must make sure of that.

You can do this with stretch film, straps, a suitable pallet or a combination of these. Is your load valuable, or does damage create a hazard? Then an anti-slip pallet is definitely recommended.

## When does an anti-slip pallet make sense?

* **Smooth packaging.** Boxes, trays, bags and crates that slide easily.
* **Valuable or fragile loads.** Where damage costs money straight away.
* **Hazardous substances.** Where damaged packaging is a risk to people and the environment.
* **Fast and automated processes.** On roller conveyors and with [AGVs](@guide-automation), the load must stay stable, even at higher speeds.

An added benefit: because the load stays on the pallet better, you often need less stretch film.

## How does an anti-slip pallet work?

The top deck of an anti-slip pallet contains strips of a rubber-like material (TPE). They create extra friction between pallet and load, so boxes and crates are less likely to shift. The [Ultra pallet](@ultra) also has anti-slip on the underside. As a result, the pallet itself is less likely to slide on the forks or on a roller conveyor.
