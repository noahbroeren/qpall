> **In short**
>
> * A pool pallet is not a separate type of pallet, but a pallet that is used again and again in a pool.
> * Any light, medium or heavy pallet can be a pool pallet, as long as it meets the requirements of the pool.
> * What matters is a long service life, fixed dimensions, a consistent weight and a suitable load capacity.
> * The success of a pool depends at least as much on good management as on the pallet itself.
> * For the toughest pools, we have the Ultra pallet, a heavy pallet of 1200 x 1000 mm.

## What is a pool pallet?

A pool pallet is a pallet that is part of a pool. That is a system in which pallets are used again and again, whether or not by different parties. A pool operator usually takes care of issuing, collecting, inspecting and repairing the pallets.

Pool pallets are often thought to be a separate category, apart from light, medium and heavy. That idea is understandable, but it is not correct. The requirements of a pool do not apply to one particular kind of pallet.

## Why almost every Q-Pall pallet fits in a pool

### Service life and quality

In a pool, a pallet circulates dozens or hundreds of times. Then its service life counts for more than the purchase price. Plastic pallets do not rot or splinter, and with normal use they last for many years.

Whether it is a light, medium or heavy pallet: with the right choice, every Q-Pall pallet offers the reliability a pool needs.

### Fixed size, weight and load capacity

To work well in a pool, a pallet must always have the same dimensions, the same weight and the same load capacity. That is the case with plastic pallets. We test every pallet model, so you know what it can carry.

Read more about [the load capacity of plastic pallets](@about-load).

Our light and medium pallets are already used in various pools in this way. The class is therefore no obstacle.

### Lower costs over time

Because plastic pallets last a long time, a pool has to replace or repair fewer pallets. Pallets with fixed dimensions also make it easier to manage and track pallets within the pool. Over time, that adds up to real savings.

### Good management matters just as much

The success of a pool depends not only on the pallets, but also on how the pool is managed. A well-managed pool has less loss and damage, faster circulation and lower costs for all participants. Together with partners, we also help think through how a pool is managed.

### Recycling at the end

A plastic pallet lasts much longer than an average wooden pallet. We make our standard pallets from recycled plastic, a mix of HDPE (2) and PP (5), without steel or other materials.

At the end of their life, we take them back and grind them into raw material for new pallets. Read more about our approach to [sustainability](@about-sustainability).

## The Ultra pallet: made for pools

For the toughest cycles, we have the [Ultra pallet](@ultra). It is a heavy pallet of 1200 x 1000 x 165 mm with anti-slip on the top and bottom. It carries up to 1,375 kg in racking without metal reinforcements, and is made for open and closed pools.

Does your pool use the Euro pallet size? Then choose a heavy pallet of 1200 x 800 mm.

## Choose the pallet that suits your pool

The idea that pool pallets are a separate category does not do justice to what pallets can do.

Every Q-Pall pallet, whether light, medium or heavy, can work in a pool, as long as it suits the load, the processes and the desired service life. It all comes down to the right choice and good management. Read more about pooling on [plastic pallets for pooling](@sector-pooling).
