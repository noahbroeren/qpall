## What is sustainable business?

Sustainability plays an increasingly important role in business. Making a profit remains important, but not at any cost. In sustainable business, or corporate social responsibility, a company takes human, natural and economic aspects into account: People, Planet and Profit.

Our production processes and the material we use fit this way of doing business. Below, you can read how we safeguard the sustainability of our plastic pallets.

## Less CO₂ through local production

Producing and transporting pallets uses energy. Burning fossil fuels such as coal, oil and gas releases CO₂. This greenhouse gas contributes to global warming.

In the pallet industry, most CO₂ emissions come from transport. A truck full of pallets mainly carries air. That is why we place a pallet’s mould as close to the customer as possible. This way, the pallets themselves do not have to travel long distances.

We produce in five factories: in Balk, Ribe, Marktoberdorf, Nantes and Lleida. Almost every customer is within 600 kilometres of a Q-Pall factory. That saves a lot of kilometres, CO₂ emissions and transport costs. Read more about [local production](@about-local).

## Pallets made of recycled plastic

Another important point is reducing plastic waste. The ‘plastic soup’ in the oceans threatens animals, plants and coral. Plastic pallets are sustainable in several ways, not least because the plastic is easy to reuse.

That is why recycling plays a major role at Q-Pall. When the company was founded in 2005, the aim was to reuse 90% of the plastic. That target has been comfortably exceeded: we make our standard pallets from nearly 100% recycled plastic, a mix of HDPE and PP.

In 2025, we processed 34,500 tonnes of recycled plastic, around 1,475 full truckloads. At the end of their life, our pallets are fully recyclable. We also support projects that raise awareness of the plastic waste problem.

## Lower consumption in production

In production, too, we keep an eye on our ecological footprint. Insight into that footprint helps us to further improve the environmental performance of our pallets. We set the bar high with strict design targets for energy and material efficiency.

For new pallets, we focus mainly on:

* **Lightweight design.** Less material is needed per pallet.
* **Less material loss.** We limit rejects and losses in production.
* **Reuse of parts and production scrap.** Leftover material goes back into the process.

This reduces water and energy consumption, material use, CO₂ emissions and waste. Our [building in Veghel](@news-hq), home to our head office, European operations centre and storage, also has a heat pump and solar panels, among other things.

## Independently assessed

We have our sustainability performance independently assessed by EcoVadis. In April 2023, we achieved the [silver medal](@news-ecovadis-silver). The bronze medal followed in 2025, and we achieved it again in October 2026. This places us in the top 35% of assessed companies. Read more on our [sustainability](@about-sustainability) page.
