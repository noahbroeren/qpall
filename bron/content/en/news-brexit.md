> **In short**
>
> * The UK left the EU on 31 January 2020. The transition period ran until 31 December 2020.
> * Since then, wooden pallets in trade between the EU and the UK have had to comply with ISPM 15: treated and stamped.
> * Plastic pallets are not covered by ISPM 15 and need no treatment or stamp.

## From referendum to departure

On 23 June 2016, the United Kingdom voted to leave the European Union. The UK left the EU on 31 January 2020. A transition period followed until 31 December 2020, during which the old rules still applied.

Since the end of that transition period, the UK has been a country outside the EU for trade purposes. That affects the goods, but also the pallets they travel on.

## What Brexit means for wooden pallets

Wooden packaging in international transport is subject to the ISPM 15 standard. The standard exists to protect plant health: insects and plant diseases must not cross borders in wood. A wooden pallet must therefore be treated, usually with heat, and carry an official stamp.

Within the EU, ISPM 15 is usually not mandatory. Since Brexit, however, the standard does apply to wooden pallets in trade between the EU and the UK, in both directions. A pallet without the correct treatment or stamp can lead to delays, return of the shipment or even its destruction.

In the run-up to Brexit, there were fears that British exporters would not have enough treated wooden pallets. Many of the pallets circulating between the UK and the EU did not comply with ISPM 15. For companies on both sides of the Channel, that was a real risk.

## Plastic pallets: no ISPM 15 needed

ISPM 15 only applies to packaging material made of solid wood. Plastic pallets are not covered by it. They need no heat treatment and no stamp, wherever your shipment is going. For a full explanation, read [ISPM 15: export without a stamp](@guide-ispm).

What this means for you in trade with the UK:

* **No treatment or certificate.** You do not have to arrange anything for the pallet itself.
* **No delays caused by the pallet.** A plastic pallet will not be rejected for a missing or incorrect stamp.
* **Light and often nestable.** Our [export pallets](@type-export) carry up to 2,500 kg static and nest inside each other when empty.
* **The right size.** The [1200 x 1000 mm](@size-1200x1000) industrial pallet is common in the UK.
* **Circular.** Our standard pallets are made from nearly 100% recycled plastic and are fully recyclable at the end of their life.
